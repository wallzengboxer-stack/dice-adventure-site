Fate Dial Adventure

A pocket strategy chase for iPhone. Spin the two-ring Fate Dial, catch the lying imp, read the hunter's footprints, and send your Champion to lure the hunters away. At the castle the dial becomes a Battle Dial: read the Demon King's telegraphed attack and strike his weak spot before the doom countdown ends.
