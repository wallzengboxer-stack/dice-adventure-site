Privacy Policy — Dice Adventure

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Dice Adventure ("the app", "we") is a single-player game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles.

We do not collect your personal information

  • The app does not require an account or login.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to us or to third parties.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking software, and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, settings (such as language and difficulty), achievements, daily/weekly challenge progress and statistics on your device only, using the system's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Sharing

If you tap "Share" after finishing a run, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text summary of your run. You choose whether and where to send it. We do not receive it.

Speech and sound

Sound effects and music are generated on your device. Some voice lines use the system's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

隐私政策（中文）

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

《Dice Adventure》是一款 iPhone 单人游戏。本政策说明应用会处理哪些信息。

我们不收集你的个人信息。不需要账号或登录；应用不联网，不会向我们或任何第三方发送数据；没有广告、统计或追踪工具，没有内购；不会跨其他公司的应用或网站追踪你。

保存在你设备上的数据：游戏进度、设置（语言、难度等）、成就、每日/每周挑战进度和统计，只保存在你的设备上，不会离开设备。删除应用即清除。

分享：通关后点"分享"，会打开 iOS 系统分享面板，带一段简短的战绩文字，是否发送由你决定，我们收不到。

声音：音效和音乐在设备上生成；部分语音使用系统自带的文字转语音，在设备上运行。

儿童：应用不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。
