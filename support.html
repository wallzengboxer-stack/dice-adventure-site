Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Open "Game Help" on the title screen. Roll the dice, move along the path and stay ahead of the shadow. Every special tile gives you a choice.

What do ⚡ and 💤 next to "Darkness" mean?
They show the shadow's next move: ⚡ it sprints 2 extra tiles, 💤 it hesitates.

How do I unlock more maps?
Clear any map once, on any difficulty, to unlock the next. Clear all 10 to reveal a hidden eleventh realm.

Does the game need an internet connection or an account?
No. It works fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I change the language?
Tap the 🌐 button at the top right of the title screen. The game supports 14 languages.
