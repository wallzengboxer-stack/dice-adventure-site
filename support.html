Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Your first New Game is a 3-minute tutorial. You can replay it any time from Game Help (first page, "Tutorial"). During a run, the pause menu shows "This run's rules".

What do the two rings of the Fate Dial do?
The inner ring is how many steps you walk. The outer ring is your action card. The imp then offers a deal: take the card, swap to the next one, or just walk and sell the card.

Does the imp lie?
Sometimes. Watch his tells. Catching a lie pays coins.

How do I use the Champion?
He moves on his own. Tap "Follow" to keep him by your side (he guards you), or "Lure away" to send him at the chasers so you can escape.

How does the final battle work?
The dial becomes a Battle Dial. The Demon King shows his attack first: red stones get hit, the star is his weak spot, and moving farther adds run-up damage. Getting hit knocks you down and heals him. Win before the doom countdown ends.

New features keep appearing. Is that normal?
Yes. One new system unlocks each run so the game never gets overwhelming. Clearing any map once unlocks everything.

Does the game need an internet connection or an account?
No. It works fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I change the language?
Tap the 🌐 button at the top right of the title screen. The game supports 14 languages.
